    Key Principles to Remember

    Hard vs. Soft Skills: Hard skills are technical and repeatable; soft skills are adaptive and often invisible. Both cause stagnation differently.

    Soft Skills Are Hidden: The things "we don't know we don't know" are almost always soft skills—the invisible barriers to progress.

    Learn Like Steve Martin: Seek feedback relentlessly, study sources of inspiration deeply, and believe you can become anything.

    Learning Slows Time: Novel experiences and continuous learning slow our perception of time's passing—music keeps us young.

    Beginner's Mind: To keep growing, maintain the openness and willingness to struggle that beginners have naturally.
