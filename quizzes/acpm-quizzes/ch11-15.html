    Key Principles to Remember

    Three Ingredients: Every effective practice session needs a clear goal, undivided attention, and feedback. Missing any one derails learning.

    Progress = Motivation: Satisfaction comes from improving, not from reaching a destination. Keep progress flowing to stay motivated.

    Staircase Progress: Expect plateaus—they signal neural reorganization, not failure. The breakthrough is being prepared beneath the surface.

    Visualization Works: Mental practice activates similar neural circuits as physical practice. A few minutes of vivid visualization—especially before sleep—accelerates learning.

    Follow Your Feelings: Your emotional responses guide learning more reliably than intellectual analysis. Study players who move you.
