    Key Principles to Remember

    Game-Level Intervention: Learning music operates at the highest biological level—like diet and exercise—with safer and more comprehensive benefits than drugs.

    Whole-Brain Activity: No activity involves more brain tissue than musical performance. The more brain you use, the more you build and protect.

    Learn New Styles for Brain Health: If optimizing for brain health rather than mastery, learning new styles and instruments provides greater benefits than perfecting familiar ones.

    Wabi-Sabi: Imperfection is a feature, not a bug. The human element—the signs of human hands—is what makes music meaningful and connects us.
