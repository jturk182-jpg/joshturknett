    Key Principles to Remember

    Listen Often: Your brain's pattern recognition systems work beneath conscious awareness, extracting useful musical information just from listening.

    No Failure, Only Feedback: The newbie child falling on the ice outlearns the cautious adult clinging to the wall. Mistakes are essential data for rewiring.

    20–25 Minutes: Focused, distraction-free practice is enough to trigger neuroplasticity. Quality always trumps quantity.

    Motor Interference: Practicing two new motor skills within four hours of each other can undermine learning of both. Space your deliberate practice sessions.

    Practice Timing: Practice when your attention is sharpest (late morning for most). Sleep is when the brain consolidates—a brief visualization before bed reinforces learning.
