    Key Principles to Remember

    Speed Is a Byproduct: The ability to play fast comes naturally from learning skills to the point of automaticity—not from practicing fast.

    Fluency Enables Memory: When you've built sound-to-motor mappings, memorizing tunes becomes as simple as remembering a melody.

    Target Your Weaknesses: The Labyrinth Technique isolates difficult sections so you spend practice time where it matters most.

    Release the Zombie: Stage fright occurs when the conscious mind interferes with automatic skills. Let your zombie subroutines do the work.

    Visualize to Build Networks: Listening to your recordings while visualizing playing builds sound-to-motor mappings effortlessly.
