    Key Principles to Remember

    Spaced Repetition: Review memories right before you're about to forget them to flatten the forgetting curve and make them permanent.

    Diagnose Stagnation: When progress stalls, identify the root cause—inspiration, comfort zones, old habits, or depleted neural network capacity.

    Break Tab Dependency: Use the 7-step Brainjo Tune-Learnin' System to learn from notation without building print-dependent networks.

    Cross the Gap of Suck: Break it down, embrace struggle, set process goals, avoid comparison, and look backwards at how far you've come.

    Build Musical Memory: The ability to remember how tunes go is essential but often neglected. Practice it deliberately.
