    Key Principles to Remember

    The First Law: To learn to play like the masters, you must learn to play like the masters—follow their learning process, not just imitate their results.

    Fluency vs. Recitation: Know your destination. If musicians in your genre don't use sheet music, you need to develop fluency (ear-to-hand), not recitation (eye-to-hand).

    Mistakes = Growth: Your brain only changes in response to errors. Without mistakes, there's no signal to rewire. Embrace them.

    Automaticity: The purpose of practice is to provide your brain the data it needs to build neural networks that operate without conscious attention.

    Learning What Can't Be Taught: Many essential musical skills are acquired subconsciously through the right exposure, not explicit instruction.
